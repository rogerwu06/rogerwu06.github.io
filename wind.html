Wind Load Generation in STAAD Following Canadian NBCC 2020 Codes

Generates grouped STAAD GX/GZ member loads from node geometry, member connectivity, section properties, and wind parameters.

Initial Design Note

This tool is intended for initial design. It uses a conservative worst-case treatment for both Cn/Ct direction and section depth/width when selecting member loading. Components that are not represented in the STAAD model, such as piping or cable trays, require separate consideration.
